WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Scripture, Burning for Revival

Learn about our core mission, our vision, and the foundational beliefs that guide our spiritual community.

Our Mission

To labor in prayer and the Word, raising a generation of sincere believers who walk in the authentic power, holiness, and love of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To see a sweeping spiritual revival across our city and nation, bringing lost souls into saving grace and deepening the fellowship of the saints.

Our Core Beliefs

  • The Authority of Scripture: The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for Christian faith and conduct.
  • The Trinity: We believe in one God eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Salvation through Christ: Redemption is received solely by grace through faith in the atoning death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  • The Power of the Holy Spirit: We believe in the active, ongoing work of the Holy Spirit transforming believers and empowering them for holy living and witness.
